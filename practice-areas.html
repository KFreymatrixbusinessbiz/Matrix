Operational Solutions

The requirement comes first.

Matrix understands the operation, balances what matters, designs the environment, and then selects technology and support that fit.

01

Operational Infrastructure

Build around the work that must continue.

Customer requirement: Technology, workflow, and support must function as a dependable operating environment.

Matrix role: Examine the work, dependencies, consequences, and constraints; design and implement an environment around them.

Technology enables: Appropriate capacity, placement, connection, visibility, recovery, and support.

Responsibility remains: Matrix supports the environment it establishes and helps reassess it as conditions change.

02

Information Control

Connected technology should fit the organization’s control requirements.

Customer requirement: Information, access, configuration, service, and physical output must be handled consistently with the organization’s responsibilities.

Matrix role: Identify where a proposed output or workplace-technology design affects exposure, access, custody, continuity, or accountability.

Technology enables: Configuration and workflow choices that can support the customer’s established security and compliance requirements.

Responsibility remains: Matrix remains accountable for the fit of its recommendation. The customer retains responsibility for its broader security and compliance program.

03

Visual Communications

Choose the form that helps people understand and act.

Customer requirement: Information must be visible, understandable, and useful where decisions or actions occur.

Matrix role: Understand the communication need and evaluate how visual technology fits the location, audience, and workflow.

Technology enables: Appropriate displays, large-format output, labeling, and related visual systems.

Responsibility remains: Matrix supports the recommended technology and helps reconsider fit when the communication requirement changes.

04

Operational Continuity

Decide where recovery can begin and where escalation belongs.

Customer requirement: The operation needs an appropriate response when technology or workflow is interrupted.

Matrix role: Examine dependency, internal capability, external support, operating hours, geography, and consequence; help define a supportable boundary.

Technology enables: Visibility, guided action, replaceable elements, automation, remote resources, or specialist support where appropriate.

Responsibility remains: Capability may be distributed, but Matrix remains accountable for the recommendation and support beyond the defined boundary.

05

Federal & Regulated Environments

Let established requirements shape the design.

Customer requirement: Technology and support must operate within applicable organizational, contractual, security, access, and documentation requirements.

Matrix role: Understand the requirements provided by the customer and evaluate how its recommended environment can support them.

Technology enables: Platform, configuration, workflow, access, and support choices appropriate to the defined operating requirement.

Responsibility remains: Matrix is accountable for its recommendation and implementation. It does not certify the customer’s compliance or replace the customer’s governing authority.

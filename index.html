A balanced operating decision

There is no single measure of the right environment.

Only your operation tells us what must be balanced.

Continuity, economics, security and control, usability, workflow, internal capability, external support, technology requirements, and operating constraints all affect the decision.

Matrix balances those requirements according to the customer’s operation rather than optimizing one universal measure.

The product is part of the answer. The operating environment is the decision.

Information responsibility

Information does not stop requiring protection when it leaves the screen.

Printed, scanned, copied, transmitted, stored, or serviced information remains part of the organization’s responsibility.

Matrix helps organizations examine the output environment as part of that information’s custody and operational infrastructure—not merely as office equipment.

Accountability over time

Matrix remains responsible for the environment it helps establish.

Responsibility does not mean Matrix must perform every action or guarantee every result. It means we remain accountable for the design, implementation, support structure, recovery boundaries, and continued fit of the recommendation.

The environment may use customer capability, Matrix capability, platform resources, specialized expertise, automation, and defined escalation. Matrix designs, implements, supports, and helps adapt that environment as conditions change.

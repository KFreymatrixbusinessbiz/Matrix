Make a Payment

Pay an Invoice by Credit Card

To request a secure credit-card payment link, email Matrix Accounts Payable at accountspayable@matrixbusiness.biz with the following information:

  1. Invoice Number(s)
    List the invoice number(s) you wish to pay.
  2. Authorized Payment Amount
    Include the total invoice amount plus the applicable credit-card processing fee.
  3. Processing Date
    Specify the date you would like the credit-card transaction processed.

Credit-card payments include a 3% processing fee (4% for American Express). The processing fee is calculated using the Total Due shown on the invoice(s).

Please do not include credit-card numbers or other card details in your email. Matrix will send a secure payment link separately.

What happens next

  1. Email the requested invoice and payment information to Matrix.
  2. Matrix sends you a secure payment link.
  3. Complete your payment using that secure link.
  4. A payment confirmation is provided after the transaction is processed.

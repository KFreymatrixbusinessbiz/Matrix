Epson Large-Format Printing

Choose the production system around the finished work.

Matrix helps organizations select Epson large-format technology by application, media, color, throughput, workflow, and the operational consequences of missed output.

Application First

Large format is not one category of work.

A technical drawing, a retail poster, and a fine-art print may share dimensions while demanding entirely different media, color, handling, and production workflows.

01

Technical & CAD

Architectural plans, engineering drawings, construction documents, GIS, maps, and technical output where line quality, scale, speed, and media handling matter.

02

Posters & signage

Retail, education, events, wayfinding, presentations, and internal communications where visual impact and responsive production matter.

03

Photography & fine art

Color-managed photographic, proofing, gallery, and art reproduction workflows where gamut, tonal control, media, and consistency are central.

04

Production graphics

Higher-volume commercial and graphics applications requiring repeatability, throughput, roll handling, finishing coordination, and predictable operation.

Define the production requirement.

Output

Finished size, image quality, line detail, color expectations, durability, viewing distance, and use environment.

Media

Roll or sheet workflow, width, thickness, coating, specialty materials, loading, cutting, take-up, and storage.

Throughput

Daily demand, peak periods, turnaround expectations, unattended operation, redundancy, and the consequence of delay.

What we help you decide.

  • The Epson platform and width appropriate to the application
  • Ink set, color management, resolution, speed, and quality priorities
  • Roll handling, cutting, take-up, finishing, and workspace requirements
  • Drivers, RIP software, design tools, file preparation, and workflow integration
  • Operator responsibilities, maintenance, consumables, support, and production continuity
  • When one system is sufficient and when the operation needs backup capacity

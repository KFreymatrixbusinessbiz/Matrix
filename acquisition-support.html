Acquisition & Support

A sound decision includes what happens after installation.

Matrix helps connect equipment selection with acquisition, configuration, deployment, supplies, service, and lifecycle expectations.

Choose the operating relationship as carefully as the product.

Acquire appropriately

Purchase and lease options considered in the context of useful life, flexibility, cash flow, and organizational preference.

Deploy correctly

Configuration, connectivity, user preparation, and implementation planned around the environment.

Support the lifecycle

Supplies, service, changes, accountability, and replacement planning established before they become urgent.

Questions worth answering early.

  • Who owns installation, configuration, user readiness, and ongoing support?
  • What happens when equipment, supplies, software, or outside service is unavailable?
  • How should cost, convenience, control, and continuity be balanced?
  • Can the support model reduce dependency without creating a second job internally?

Define the relationship before signing the agreement.

We can help you compare the equipment, acquisition structure, and support model as one decision.

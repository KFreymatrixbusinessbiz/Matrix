Responsibility

Matrix is accountable for the environment it helps establish.

We begin by understanding how the work operates, then determine what technology and support structure fit the requirements. Matrix designs the environment, implements it, supports it, and remains responsible for continuing judgment about its fit.

Accountability does not mean Matrix performs every action. An appropriate environment may use customer capability, Matrix capability, manufacturer or platform resources, specialized expertise, automation, and defined escalation.

It also is not a guarantee of uptime, savings, security, compliance, or every business result. It is a commitment to remain answerable for the recommendation, its operating boundaries, and the support structure around it.
